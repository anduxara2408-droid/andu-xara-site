    Lesson 3: Greetings

     Salaamu aleykum                        Peace be upon you (use at any
                                            time of the day)
     Aleykum salaam                         And on you, too (reply to the above
                                            greeting)
     An wuyi jam                            Good morning

     Lella                                  Good afternoon

     Sunka                                  Good evening

     Xori an wa jam?                        Are you in peace?

     Jam baane                              Peace only

     An moxo?                               How are you?

     Ma jam                                 I am fine

     Ka-dunko n moxo?                       How is the family?

     I wa jam                               They are fine

     Golle n moxo?                          How is the work?

     Jam duuro                              It is fine

     An do tanpiye n moxo?                  How are you with tiredness?

     Ma jam                                 I am fine

     Mary moxo?                             How is Mary?

     Mary wa jam                            Mary is fine

     Leminu n moxo?                         How are your children?

     I wa jam                               They are fine

     Bisimilla!                             Welcome!

     Nawaari                                Thank you!

     Lesson 8: Introducing Self/Someone

      N toxo n ni Souleymane                 My name is Souleymane

      N janmu n ni Sall                      My last name is Sall

      An toxo?                               What’s your name?

      An janmu?                              What’s your last name?

      N bakka Moritani n ya                  I am from Mauritania

      N yaqqe n bakka Moritani n ya          My wife is from Mauritania

      An bakka minna?                        Where are you from?

      An yaqqe n bakka minna?                Where is your wife from?

      A bakka minna?                         Where is she from?

      A bakka minna?                         Where is he from?

      A toxo n ni John                       His name is John

      A janmu n ni Hogan                     His last name is Hogan

      John feti Moritaninke.                 John is not from Mauritania. He
      Amerikinke n ya ni                     is from America
      Amerikinke n ya ni an qa?              Are you from America?

      Ayi, Amerikinke feti in qa             No, I am not from America.

      Moritaninke n ya ni an qa?             Are you from Mauritania?

      Iyo, Moritaninke n ya ni in qa         Yes, I am from Mauritania.

      Xaranmoxe n ya ni in qa                I am a teacher

      N yaqqe n feti xaranmoxe               My wife is not a teacher

      John ni wolonteeri n ya                John is a volunteer

      A yaqqe n feti wolonteeri              His wife is not a volunteer

      John yaqqe n ni doktooru n ya          John’s wife is a doctor

     Lesson 9: Leave-taking Expressions

      Hari na o koyi me!                     Good-bye!

      O na me wari jam!                      See you!

      Jam na kira                            Have a good day

      Jam na wuyi                            Have a good night

      Hari na xeeri ro wuro n qa             May we have a good night

      Na jam dallandi                        Stay in peace

      Anmiina!                               Amen!

      O wa suxuba qa                         See you in the morning

      O wa lelle qa                          See you in the afternoon

      O wa kinsiga qa                        See you around noon

      O wa wuro qa                           See you in the evening

      O wa xunbane                           See you tomorrow

      O n katta kaane                        See you soon

      O n katta kaane                        See you later

     Lesson 10: Some Useful Expressions & Phrases

      N tanpi                                I am tired

      Xenqo n wa in qa                       I am sleepy

      Dulle n wa in qa                       I am hungry

      Dulle nta in qa                        I am not hungry

      Daqqu n wa in qa                       I am thirsty

      Daqqu nta in qa                        I am not thirsty

      A tewe n ya ni                         It’s hot

      A tewe fe                              It’s not hot

      A mulle n ya ni                        It’s cold

      A mulle fe                             It’s not cold

      Haqqe toxo                             Excuse me

      Haqqe nta an qa                        You are excused

      An telle minna?                        Where are you going?

      N telle o ka n ya                      I am going home

      N telle xaran-ra n ya                  I am going to school

      John wa telle saxa                     John is going to the market

      John wa telle butiki                   He is going to the shop

      N wa telle biro                        I’m not going to the office

      Iyo, n wa Soninke n mukku              Yes, I speak Soninke a little bit
